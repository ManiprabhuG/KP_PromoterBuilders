How We Collect, Protect & Respect Your Personal Information

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: July 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Kopuram Promoter and Builders LLP, we prioritize the privacy and security of our clients. We collect personal details only when you voluntarily submit them through our website forms, site visit booking system, brochure downloads, or direct inquiries. This information may include:

  • Your Full Name and Phone Number / WhatsApp contact
  • Email address
  • Preferred plot layout project and budget requirements
  • Requested site visit date and time slot

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is strictly used for legitimate real estate communication purposes, including:

  • Scheduling and confirming your free AC cab site visit
  • Sending digital project brochures, layout maps, and DTCP/RERA approval details
  • Providing project updates, price schedules, and bank loan assistance
  • Responding to your specific property inquiries

3. Data Non-Disclosure & Security Guarantee

We never sell, rent, trade, or leak your personal contact details to third-party telemarketers or external agencies. Your data is stored securely and accessed strictly by authorized relationship managers of Kopuram Promoter and Builders LLP.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes standard browser localStorage and sessionStorage to store your language preference (English/Tamil), dark mode settings, and popup dismiss states to enhance your browsing experience.
