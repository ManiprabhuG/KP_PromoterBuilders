2. Plot Layout Approvals & Legal Compliance

All land layouts promoted by Kopuram Promoter and Builders LLP strictly comply with government DTCP (Directorate of Town and Country Planning) and RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) standards. Complete legal documentation, Parent Deeds, EC (Encumbrance Certificates), and Approved Layout Maps are made available to prospective buyers prior to sale deed registration.

3. Site Visit & Plot Reservation Rules

  • Free Site Visits: Free AC cab pickup facility is provided for family site visits within Madurai limits subject to prior booking slot confirmation.
  • Plot Reservation: Plots are reserved on a first-come, first-served basis upon receipt of an advance booking deposit.
  • Pricing: Land prices per square foot are fixed as per current project price schedules and do not include statutory government registration charges or stamp duty fees.

Buyers are required to adhere to the mutually agreed payment schedule upon plot booking. Final sale deed registration is executed upon receipt of full plot payment.

We provide end-to-end guidance for prompt Patta transfer, revenue mutation, and individual plot boundary demarcation following successful registration.
