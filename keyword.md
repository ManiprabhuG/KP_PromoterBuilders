# keyword.md

# OBJECTIVE

Analyze the business, industry, services, products, target audience, competitors, and location to identify the most valuable SEO keywords that can generate organic traffic, leads, inquiries, and conversions.

Use trusted SEO data sources and keyword research methodologies, including:

* Google Trends
* Google Keyword Planner
* Google Search Suggestions
* Google Related Searches
* Google Search Console (if connected)
* Bing Keyword Research
* Ahrefs (if available)
* SEMrush (if available)
* Ubersuggest
* Moz
* AnswerThePublic
* AlsoAsked
* Keyword Surfer
* Industry-specific keyword sources

Do not guess keywords.

Use actual trend analysis and keyword research wherever possible.

## Search Intent

Categorize keywords into:

### Informational

Users seeking information.

Example:

* What is DTCP approval
* Benefits of buying plots

---

### Commercial

Users comparing options.

Example:

* Best builders in Madurai
* Top plot promoters in Madurai

---

### Transactional

Users ready to take action.

Example:

* Buy plots in Madurai
* Book site visit Madurai

---

### Navigational

Users searching for a specific company.

Example:

* Company Name
* Company Reviews
* Company Contact
